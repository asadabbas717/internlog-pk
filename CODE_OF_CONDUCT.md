# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome different backgrounds and experience levels. Critique code and ideas constructively; respect privacy and use synthetic examples.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing and disclosure of private student information are unacceptable. Stop harmful behavior when asked and cooperate with fair moderation.

For visible violations, use the hosting platform's reporting tools. For sensitive reports, request a private maintainer channel without publishing personal details; no dedicated contact is established yet. Maintainers may remove harmful content, request changes, or restrict participation proportionately. Anyone involved in a report should avoid retaliation and conflicts of interest. This policy applies to project spaces and representation of the project.
